August 1st
My dearest love,

I know it's a made-up holiday. Most of the good ones are. But I like that there's a day whose only job is to say: I noticed. I noticed the way you laugh at your own jokes before you finish telling them. I noticed how you remember things I forgot I told you. I noticed that ordinary days are better simply because you're in them.

So today isn't about grand gestures. It's about saying, plainly, that I'm glad I get to do life next to you — the boring parts and the good parts and the parts we haven't gotten to yet.

Always yours, me
